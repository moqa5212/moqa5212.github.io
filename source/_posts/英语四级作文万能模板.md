### 英语四级作文万能模板：词汇、句型、结构、范文一篇备全

> 四级作文想要拿高分，靠的不是临场灵光一现，而是平时把"词汇、句型、结构"三样东西练到能条件反射。

这篇文章把四级作文最核心的几块内容系统地整理在一起：从同义替换、逻辑衔接词，到高阶句型、论证方式，再到议论文、书信、报告三大文体的固定结构，最后附上一篇可直接背诵的真题范文。

建议的用法是：**先背模板和句型，再用真题套写 2–3 篇**，把别人的句子真正变成自己的表达。下面进入正题。

---

### 一、核心词汇积累

写作想摆脱"简单词、口水话"，第一步就是有意识地换词。下面这些替换词建议重点记。

#### 1. 基础同义替换

| 想表达 | 可以替换成 |
| :--- | :--- |
| 认为 | think、believe、**claim**、**assert**、contend、argue、assume、presume |
| 人们 | people、**individuals**、students、young men、adults |
| 喜欢 | like、be fond of、**be keen on**、**have a passion for** |
| 重要 | important、**significant**、**crucial**、vital、essential |

#### 2. 逻辑衔接词

一篇作文之所以显得"散"，往往是缺少衔接。按功能把下面几类词用熟，行文立刻顺畅起来。

| 关系 | 常用表达 |
| :--- | :--- |
| 因果 | 原因：because、in that、as、since、because of、**due to**、**owing to**、thanks to；结果：so、hence、**therefore**、thereby、thus、**consequently**、in consequence、as a result |
| 并列 | and、likewise、**similarly**、equally、simultaneously、at the same time、in the meanwhile |
| 对比 | but、**by contrast**、conversely、on the contrary、**on the other hand**、while、whereas |
| 递进 | besides、also、**in addition**、**moreover**、furthermore、apart from this |
| 让步 | despite、in spite of、although、though、even if、**much as**、granted that |
| 引出新对象 | concerning、regarding、**with regard to**、as for、**when it comes to**、in terms of |
| 总结 | **on the whole**、all in all、**in conclusion**、generally speaking、**to sum up**、in a word、in short |

### 二、万能句型重点知识

#### 1. 被动句

写作文时，如果主语总是"人/人称代词"，会显得句式单一。把这类句子改成被动，句式立刻高级。

> 原则：出现"人们/大家/我们……"作主语时，优先考虑改成被动句。

* 例句：人们普遍认为网购已经成为一种流行趋势。
* **Online shopping is argued to be a popular trend among the young and the old.**

#### 2. There be 句型

用于表达"存在/有"，是开头段和论述段的通用句式。

* 目前关于孩子和父母之间的关系问题有一个激烈的讨论。
  **There is a hot debate about the relationship between parents and their children.**
* 毫无疑问，线上词典在大学生中越来越受欢迎。
  **There is no denying that online dictionaries are becoming increasingly popular with college students.**

#### 3. It 结构

**（1）it 作形式主语**（把从句或不定式后置，避免头重脚轻）：

经典模板，一篇作文用 1 次即可，用来保证句式多样：

* It is common knowledge that…（……是常识）
* It is my belief that…（我认为……）
* It is self-evident that…（……显而易见）
* **It is universally acknowledged that…**（人们普遍认为……）
* **It is beyond dispute that…**（……毋庸置疑）

例句：大学教育极大地促进了社会进步，这一点是显而易见的。
**It is beyond dispute that university education has greatly promoted the progress of our society.**

**（2）强调句型**：`It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who…`，用来突出重点。

* 强调地点：It was in the street that I met an old friend yesterday.
* 强调主语：**It was the Silk Road that gradually became a major road connecting the East and the West.**

#### 4. 复合句

**（1）同位语 / 同位语从句**（补充说明名词，扩写句子）：

* 名词作同位语：**Garbage sorting, a necessary behavior to control pollution, proves quite crucial.**
* 经典句型：**The fact that… suggests that…**（……的事实表明……，分析原因专用）

例句：经常锻炼能让人精力充沛，这个事实表明我们应多运动。
**The fact that doing sports regularly can make you energetic suggests that we should take more exercise.**

**（2）定语从句**（which/who 引导，衔接两个句子）：

* 非限制性定语从句（修饰整句话，译"这"）：
  **Chinese celebrate harvest in the Mid-Autumn Festival, which is similar to Thanksgiving Day in North America.**

**（3）状语从句**（加时间/原因/让步/方式状语，拉长句子）：

> 任意句子都可加状语扩写，**状语放句首 + 逗号**是满分写法。

* 让步状语：**Although they might bring troubles, raising pets can reduce loneliness of the elderly.**
* 方式状语：**Doing sports, with their friends, can make students stronger.**

#### 5. 两大经典高分句型

**（1）虚拟语气**（正反论证、提建议专用）：

* `If` 引导虚拟（分析原因，表"假设"）：
  例句：如果沉迷暴力游戏，人们会有暴力倾向。
  **If you indulged in violent games, you would tend to be violent in life.**
* **It is necessary/urgent that…**（提措施用，should 可省略）：
  例句：大学生应多参加活动来融入新环境。
  **It is necessary that college students (should) take part in more activities.**
* **It is (high) time that…**（总结用，从句谓语用过去式或 should do）：
  **It is high time that we paid attention to this issue.**（是时候关注这个问题了）

**（2）倒装句**（表强调，阅卷老师一眼就能注意到）：

* **Only + 状语放句首**：**Only in this way can we make use of these games properly.**
* **So…that… 倒装**：**So excited was he that he couldn't help laughing.**

### 三、句子扩写与改写技巧

字数不够、句子太短，是四级作文最常见的两个问题。掌握下面两个技巧，基本就能解决。

#### 1. 扩写技巧（简单句变复杂句）

* **加同位语**：Violent games → **Violent games, a popular online pastime**
* **加定语/状语**：Reading books → **Reading books beneficial to health in their spare time**
* **加万能定语**：用 such as 举例，或用 and / as well as 并列形容词
  例句：I love pets → **I love pets such as cats, dogs and fish, cute and loyal.**

#### 2. 改写技巧（一个简单句，变出多种高级句式）

以"我绝不嫁给你"为例，一句 `I will never marry you.` 可以花式改写：

* 倒装句：**Never will I marry you.**
* 强调句：**It is you that I will never marry.**
* 主语从句：**It is beyond dispute that I will never marry you.**
* 状语从句：**I will not marry you unless the sun rises in the west.**

### 四、三大论证方式

四级作文第二段的核心是**分析原因、影响或重要性**。下面三种论证方式可任选 1–2 种组合使用。

#### 1. 因果论证

> 核心逻辑：If… → 结果一 → 结果二 → 最终结果
>
> 例句：为什么要远离暴力游戏？
> **If teenagers played violent games, they would imitate violent behaviors, becoming isolated from others. As a result, they would communicate less with people, which may lead to more crimes.**

常用因果衔接：**A results in B** / A leads to B / B is the result of A

#### 2. 分类论证

按"个人—校园—社会/国家"等维度展开，适合论述危害或重要性，内容更充实。

> 例句：人际关系中相互理解的重要性
> **In a dormitory, mutual understanding makes students more creative in study. On a campus, it contributes to a warm environment. In a country, it reduces social conflicts.**

#### 3. 举例论证

结构：**引入例子 + 具体例子 + 总结例子**，避免只罗列、不总结。

引入例子模板：

* Although there are numerous cases, the following one is the most typical.
* I can figure out no better illustration than the following one.

总结例子模板：

* This case effectively clarifies the fact that…
* The story tells us that…

例句：为什么要培养孩子直面挑战的能力？
**Although there are numerous cases, the following one is the most typical. My teacher Liu was born in a poor family and failed to enter a famous university, but she never gave up. Finally, her dream came true through hard work. This case clarifies the fact that we should develop the ability to face challenges.**

### 五、三大文体万能结构

四级作文主要考**议论文、书信、报告**三种文体。把结构固定下来，提笔就能写。

#### 1. 议论文（考查频率最高，三段式）

**第一段：引出话题 + 个人观点**（2 句即可）

引出话题（任选一句）：

* **Recently, the problem of… has been brought into focus.**
* **With the development of science and technology, the phenomenon of… has aroused wide concern.**
* **There is no doubt that the issue about… has caused public attention recently.**

表达个人观点（任选一句）：

* **As far as I am concerned, I believe that…**
* **From my perspective, …**
* **It is my belief that…**

**第二段：分析原因**（总述句 + 2–3 个具体原因）

原因总述句（任选一句）：

* The reason for this is not far to seek.（其原因不难查找）
* **There exist numerous reasons for the present phenomenon.**
* As we know, recognizing a problem is the first step in finding a solution.

具体原因：用 **First of all / In addition / Finally** 衔接，再结合上面的因果、分类或举例论证展开。

**第三段：总结 + 措施 + 倡议**（3 句话）

1. 总结句（任选一句）：
   * **In short, it is a sound conclusion that can be drawn that…**
   * **From what has been mentioned above, we can draw the conclusion that…**
   * It is my belief that…
2. 具体措施（主体建议用"个人/父母/老师"，少提政府，减少模板感）：
   * Students should be educated to learn the advantages and disadvantages of…
   * Parents should set a good example for their kids to…
3. 倡议句（用倒装或强调句收尾，提升结尾档次）：
   * **Only in this way can we develop its advantages and avoid its weaknesses.**
   * **Only by taking these actions can people have a more brilliant future.**
   * The more quickly measures are taken, the more effectively this problem will be solved.

#### 2. 书信作文

通用框架：**称呼 + 三段正文 + 落款**。

1. **称呼**
   * 公务信（给机构/陌生人）：**Dear Sir or Madam,**
   * 私人信（给朋友/同学）：Dear XX, / Dear friend,
2. **第一段**（自我介绍或问候 + 写作目的）
   * 公务信：**I am a freshman from the Department of XX in this university. The aim of my letter is to…**
   * 私人信：**How are you getting on recently? I am writing to invite you to…**
3. **第二段**：按题目要求展开（提建议、邀请、感谢等），用 **To begin with / In addition / Finally** 衔接。
4. **第三段**（感谢或祝福 + 期待回信）
   * 公务信（感谢）：**My thanks to you for your consideration are beyond words. I am looking forward to your reply.**
   * 私人信（祝福）：**Hope to see you as soon as possible. I am looking forward to your reply.**
5. **落款**（固定格式，必须写）
   * **Yours sincerely,**
   * **Li Ming**

书信常用表达：

* 感谢：**Words fail me when I want to express my sincere gratitude to you.**
* 期待回信：**I look forward to a favorable reply at your earliest convenience.**

#### 3. 报告作文（新闻报告）

三段式固定结构，简单好记：

1. **第一段**：活动**时间 + 地点 + 名称**（一句话概括）
   例句：**On June 18, a volunteer activity was organized by the Student Union in the local Nursing House.**
2. **第二段**：活动**目的 + 具体内容**（用 and / by 衔接）
   例句：**The activity was aimed at helping the elderly. Many students participated in it by making meals and chatting with the elderly.**
3. **第三段**：活动**总结/意义**（一句话收尾）
   例句：**To sum up, the activity is quite successful not only for the elderly but also for the students.**

### 六、真题范文参考

下面这几篇覆盖了大学四级最常考的议论文、书信和报告三类文体，每一篇都可以直接背诵，也可以拆开当素材套用。

| 范文 | 文体 | 话题 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 范文 1 | 议论文 | 暴力游戏是否导致暴力行为 |
| 范文 2 | 议论文 | 大学生是否该做兼职 |
| 范文 3 | 议论文 | 低碳生活的重要性 |
| 范文 4 | 书信（邀请信） | 邀请外国朋友过春节 |
| 范文 5 | 书信（申请信） | 申请成为志愿者 |
| 范文 6 | 报告 | 校园义卖活动报道 |

#### 范文 1：Whether violent video games online will cause students' violent behaviors

With the rapid development of science and technology, there is a hot debate about violent video games online. From my perspective, these games will not cause violent behaviors among teenagers.

The reasons for this are not far to seek. First of all, by playing online games, students who spend most of their time on study can relieve pressure in real life. In addition, young people know clearly that online games are virtual and the violent elements are unrealistic. As a consequence, the violence in games has little impact on real life.

To sum up, it is my belief that violent online games will not result in students' violent tendency. It is necessary that parents lead their children in the right directions. Only in this way can these kids make use of these games properly.

#### 范文 2：Should College Students Take Part-time Jobs?

Nowadays, more and more college students are taking part-time jobs in their spare time, which has aroused wide concern on campus. From my perspective, taking a part-time job is beneficial to college students as long as it is properly arranged.

The reasons for this are not far to seek. First of all, a part-time job enables students to accumulate working experience, which will make them more competitive in the future job market. In addition, by earning money on their own, students can better understand the value of labor and learn to manage their expenses. Finally, dealing with different people at work helps improve their communication skills.

In short, it is a sound conclusion that can be drawn that part-time jobs do more good than harm to college students. However, students should always put study in the first place. Only in this way can they benefit from part-time jobs without neglecting their studies.

#### 范文 3：On the Importance of a Low-carbon Life

With the rapid development of economy, environmental problems have become increasingly serious, and a low-carbon life has aroused wide concern among the public. As far as I am concerned, it is of great significance for us to lead a low-carbon life.

There exist numerous reasons for this. First of all, a low-carbon life can reduce the waste of resources and protect our environment. In addition, small actions such as saving electricity and taking public transportation not only benefit nature but also help us develop a healthy lifestyle. What's more, if everyone made a little effort, the whole society would become a better place to live in.

From what has been mentioned above, we can draw the conclusion that leading a low-carbon life is everyone's responsibility. It is high time that we took action in our daily life. Only in this way can we build a greener and more beautiful world.

#### 范文 4：A Letter of Invitation（邀请信）

Dear Tom,

How are you getting on recently? I am writing to invite you to spend the Spring Festival with my family in my hometown.

The Spring Festival is the most important traditional festival in China, and it will be a wonderful chance for you to experience Chinese culture. To begin with, you can enjoy delicious traditional food, such as dumplings and rice cakes. In addition, we will visit my grandparents and watch the dragon dance together. Finally, I will show you around my hometown and introduce some interesting customs to you.

I do hope you can come and share this special moment with us. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文 5：A Letter of Application（申请信）

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a sophomore from the Department of English in this university. The aim of my letter is to apply for the position of a volunteer for the coming International Culture Festival.

I believe I am well qualified for this job. To begin with, my excellent command of English enables me to communicate with foreign guests fluently. In addition, I have taken part in several campus activities, which has developed my ability to organize and cooperate with others. Finally, I am a responsible and warm-hearted person, and I am always ready to help others.

My thanks to you for your consideration are beyond words. I would appreciate it if you could give me this opportunity. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文 6：A Campus Activity Report（活动报道）

On June 18, a charity sale was organized by the Student Union on the campus playground.

The activity was aimed at raising money for children in poor areas. Many students took part in it by donating books, clothes and handmade gifts. Besides, a lot of teachers and students came to buy things and showed great support for the activity. The money raised will be sent to the children who need help most.

To sum up, the activity is quite successful not only for the children in need but also for the students themselves. It not only helps those who need help but also teaches us the importance of giving and sharing.

### 写在最后

四级作文提分的核心，其实就三步：**背会模板、灵活套用、保证书写**。

上面这些句型和结构都是四级高频考点，建议反复背诵，再结合真题练习 2–3 篇，把模板真正用顺手。考试时，一篇作文里不必用尽所有句型，挑两三个亮点句式点缀在中后段，反而比通篇堆砌更自然、也更容易拿分。

> 考前快速过一遍这份模板，再带着真题手感上考场，作文这一项就能稳稳提分。
